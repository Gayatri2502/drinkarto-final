## Decisions for Drinkarto

- Use a state directory, a concise pricing explanation, bottle-size selection and an expandable source receipt.
- Separate geography, market model verification and actual price coverage. A listed state does not imply that current prices are connected.
- Do not reproduce blanket effective-tax percentages, “easy import”, readiness claims or universal VAT bases as legal facts.
- Do not treat permit exceptions or partial prohibition as a nationwide prohibition rule.
- Preserve source dates, product codes and units. Never add case-level excise to a final bottle MRP a second time.
- Show manufacturer cost and individual margins as not published when the primary source does not disclose them.
- The Kerala receipt uses published case and FL-1 bottle columns. Any reconciliation difference is derived and is explicitly labelled as such.
- All remaining per-state rates and formula details require the relevant current government notification before activation.
