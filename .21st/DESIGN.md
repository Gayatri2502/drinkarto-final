# Drinkarto public design context

Warm paper, ink and olive. Editorial serif display type, practical sans serif body type. Restrained borders, small radii and deliberate photographic compositions replace layered glass panels and unrelated card colours.

The public design tokens live in `src/app/(public)/editorial.css`; ERP tokens remain separate. StateLens leads with the bottle comparison and defers the nationwide rules directory to a disclosure. Missing prices remain unavailable.

Motion is brief and purposeful: sliding navigation, a once-only viewport entrance, and a subtle bottle hover. All respect reduced motion. Content stays visible without motion JavaScript.

21st references retrieved on 24 October 2026: [Animated Tabs by educalvolpz](https://21st.dev/@educalvolpz/components/animated-tabs), [Reveal by asanshay](https://21st.dev/@asanshay/components/reveal). These interaction patterns are independently adapted to this plain-CSS React codebase with no Motion or Tailwind dependency. Existing Works Wheel remains in use. No theme was published and no hosted AI generation was invoked.
